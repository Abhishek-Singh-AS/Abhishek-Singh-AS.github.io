About Me

I'm an AI/ML and Generative AI developer who loves turning ideas into products that actually work. My projects range from retrieval-augmented generation apps and deep learning models to speech AI and automation tools.

I work mainly with Python, FastAPI and LangChain, and I enjoy taking a project end to end: designing the model or pipeline, building the API and interface, and deploying it so people can use it.
